Onboarding Preparation Checklist

To build an accurate wealth blueprint, we will require details of your current finances. Prepare these documents for your second session:

**Existing Portfolios:** CAS statement of mutual funds (PDF format) and stock broker holdings.
**Insurance Policies:** Policy premium schedule documents for active life and health term plans.
**Income Details:** Copy of recent Form 16 and ITR filing details.
**Debt details:** Interest rate and balance schedules of active home or vehicle loans.
Commission Alert

How Commissions Erode Your Wealth

Regular mutual funds charge an extra **1% to 1.5% commission** every single year that goes directly to agents as trails. Over a 20-year cycle, this single drag can eat up to **30% of your total retirement corpus**!

Regular Mutual Fund (1.8% Expense)

₹10 Lakhs becomes ₹58L in 15 yrs

Direct Mutual Fund (0.5% Expense)

₹10 Lakhs becomes ₹71L in 15 yrs

Calculation assumes an underlying equity index return of 14% p.a. compounded yearly.
