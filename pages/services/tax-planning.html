Maximize Post-Tax Wealth With Legal Structuring

Tax planning is not about evading taxes; it is the strategic method of leveraging legal provisions (deductions, exemptions, rebates) to minimize your net tax liabilities. Every rupee saved in income tax is a rupee added directly to your investment pool, compounding over time. Proper salary and capital gains structuring is key to boosting your net worth.

We help salaried and business professionals analyze their compensation structures, evaluate slabs under both old and new regimes, and set up automatic tax-harvesting schedules.

Tax Planning FAQs

The New Regime offers lower tax rates but eliminates almost all standard deductions (except Section 80CCD(2) for corporate NPS). The Old Regime permits deductions like HRA, home loans, Section 80C, and 80D. We run comparison models on both regimes based on your actual assets and interest payments to determine the lowest tax channel.

Under Indian tax laws, long-term capital gains (LTCG) on equities/mutual funds are exempt up to ₹1.25 Lakhs per financial year. Tax harvesting involves selling mutual funds annually to book gains under this threshold, then immediately buying them back, raising your purchase cost basis and saving future capital gains tax.
