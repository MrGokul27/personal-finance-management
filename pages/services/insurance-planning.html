Separate Insurance From Investment

The single biggest mistake in personal finance is mixing investment with insurance. Traditional endowment policies, ULIPs, and money-back plans charge high premium expenses but yield extremely poor returns (~5%-6%) and offer inadequate life cover. Insurance is a pure risk hedge, not a wealth generator. Protecting your family requires pure term cover and comprehensive medical insurance.

We conduct objective insurance evaluations to size your Human Life Value (HLV) and audit existing policies. We do not sell insurance policies, assuring completely conflict-free reviews.

Core Hedges We Structure

Pure Term Life Insurance

Sizing pure life coverage up to 15-20 times your annual income (Human Life Value method) with no maturity returns.

Health & Super Topup Setup

Building a robust medical cover (e.g. ₹10L base + ₹90L super topup) to protect against massive hospital bills.

Critical Illness Riders

Lump-sum payouts upon diagnosis of critical conditions like cancer or cardiovascular diseases.

Personal Accident Covers

Hedges for permanent or temporary disability to cover loss of income and recurring expenses.

Case Study Highlight

Restructuring Vikram's Legacy LIC Endowment Policies

**Client Profile:** Vikram, 35, paid ₹1.8 Lakhs annually for 4 traditional endowment and money-back policies, giving a total life cover of only ₹15 Lakhs.

**Our Solution:** Recommended surrendering 3 policies and making 1 paid-up. Replaced them with a pure Term Life cover of ₹2 Crores costing only ₹24,000 annually. Redirected the remaining ₹1.56 Lakhs to index mutual funds.

Life cover increased from ₹15L to ₹2Cr Annual surplus for investments: ₹1.56 Lakhs
