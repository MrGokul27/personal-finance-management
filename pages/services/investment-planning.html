Investment Planning FAQs

Regular mutual funds include a trailing brokerage commission (usually 1-1.5%) paid to distributors every year. Direct mutual funds bypass this agent fee, allowing you to invest directly with the fund house and earn higher returns.

We audit portfolios quarterly but only execute rebalancing transactions if target allocations drift by more than 5%, or once a year during annual tax harvesting cycles.
